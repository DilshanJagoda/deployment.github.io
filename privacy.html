Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy explains how MURA — the official website of Murshad Huvais (MURA) — collects and handles information when you visit this site or use the booking form.

Information We Collect

  • Booking form data: your name, phone number, show type, event date, location, audience size and any details you enter when booking a show.
  • Basic usage data: standard web analytics such as pages viewed and browser type, if analytics is enabled.

How We Use Your Information

Booking information is used solely to respond to your enquiry. When you submit the booking form, your request is opened in WhatsApp — it is sent directly to MURA's WhatsApp number and is not stored on any server by this website.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies. Any third-party embeds (such as Spotify players) may set their own cookies according to their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Links

This site links to external platforms including WhatsApp, Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to request that we remove or correct any personal information you provided. Since booking data is sent via WhatsApp, you can simply delete the chat on your end as well.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, reach out via the WhatsApp button on the website or through any of the social media accounts listed on the site.
